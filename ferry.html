Boarding Information

Jack Layton Ferry Terminal

Located at the foot of Bay Street at 9 Queens Quay West, the Jack Layton Ferry Terminal is Toronto's primary departure gate to the islands.

🚇
TTC Subway & Streetcar: Take Line 1 to Union Station. From Union Station, transfer to the 509 Harbourfront or 510 Spadina streetcar south to Queens Quay, or enjoy a 7-minute walk south down Bay Street.
🚗
Parking: Underground public parking garages are available across from the terminal at 200 Queens Quay West and Queens Quay Terminal.
♿
Accessibility: All terminal gates, boarding ramps, and ferry vessels are wheelchair and mobility device accessible.
