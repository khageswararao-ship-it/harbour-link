Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Toronto Island ferry crossings, bicycles, and island amenities.

Are dogs and pets allowed on the ferries and islands?

Yes! Dogs are welcome on all passenger ferries and on the Toronto Islands at no charge. Dogs must be kept on a standard 2-metre leash at all times, with the exception of the designated off-leash dog area located on Ward's Island.

Do ferry tickets expire if I cannot make my scheduled trip?

No! Tickets purchased online through Harbour Link are flexible round-trip passes valid for any sailing on your booked date. If weather disrupts your plans, passes may be redeemed on any subsequent day during the active operating season.

Can I return from a different island terminal than the one I arrived at?

Yes, absolutely! All three island docks (Centre Island, Ward's Island, and Hanlan's Point) are connected by paved walking and cycling trails. Your return trip is free and valid from any of the three island docks back to Jack Layton Ferry Terminal.

Are the ferry vessels wheelchair and stroller accessible?

Yes. Jack Layton Ferry Terminal and all island docks feature level ramp boarding systems. All mainline vessels offer dedicated main deck spaces for wheelchairs, mobility scooters, and strollers.
