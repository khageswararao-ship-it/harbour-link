Sports & Outdoors

Ward's Island 18-Hole Disc Golf

Toronto's premiere disc golf course winds through rolling tree lines, open meadows, and lakeshore edges on Ward's Island.

  • 🎯 18 Championship Quality Holes: Free to play all year round (bring your own discs or buy discs at the rental hut).
  • 🌲 Scenic Landscape: Mature willow trees and natural terrain challenges make it exciting for novices and pros alike.
  • 🍺 Adjacent Cafes: Refresh yourself at the Ward's Island Clubhouse or Rectory Cafe just a 5-minute walk from Hole 18.
